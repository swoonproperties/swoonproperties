How I Help Buyers

Find the right home with confidence.

From your first budget conversation to the day you collect your keys — I coordinate the search, the numbers, and the paperwork.

01

Loan & Affordability Planning

Before we shortlist anything, I help you understand your borrowing power, estimate your DSR (debt service ratio), and work out a realistic monthly commitment.

02

Tailored Home Search

Share your budget, location, and must-haves. I search both my own listings and the wider market — including off-market opportunities — to shortlist homes that actually fit.

03

Property Viewing & Advice

I arrange and accompany viewings, giving you honest, on-the-spot input on condition, value, and fit — so you decide with confidence, not guesswork.

04

Price Negotiation

I negotiate price and terms on your behalf — my incentive is aligned with getting you the best deal, not the highest price.

05

Bank Loan Submission Support

I help prepare and submit your loan application to multiple banks, comparing offers so you get competitive rates and terms.

06

Legal & Documentation Coordination

I liaise with lawyers and coordinate the Sale & Purchase Agreement process so nothing falls through the cracks.

07

End-to-End Purchase Support (until key handover)

From loan approval to key collection, I stay in the loop so you always know what's happening and what's next.
